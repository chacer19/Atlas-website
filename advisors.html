Why Advisors Choose Atlas

OUTPERFORM isn't a slogan here. It's the culture.

Every part of the Atlas platform is built around one question: what would let an advisor spend more time with clients, and less time fighting their own back office?

Institutional Infrastructure

Independent custody through Charles Schwab, in-house investment management, and a proprietary platform built for scale.

True Independence

A hybrid structure — securities through Purshe Kaplan Sterling, advisory through Ignite Planners LLC — built so you keep your book, your way.

More Time With Clients

A dedicated operations and servicing team absorbs paperwork, account opening, and transfers — so you don't have to.

A Culture Built to Last

A deep bench across leadership, advisory, operations, and business development — so no advisor is on an island.

The Atlas Model

Built for advisors who want infrastructure without giving up independence.

Atlas operates as a hybrid platform — securities offered through Purshe Kaplan Sterling Investments, advisory services through Ignite Planners LLC. That structure gives advisors the flexibility to serve clients on either the brokerage or advisory side, backed by the same institutional support either way.
